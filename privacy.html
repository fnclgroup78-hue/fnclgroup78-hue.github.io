Advisor OS — Privacy policy

Advisor OS is a private application used only by its owner, on his own computer.

Google user data

  • Access requested: read-only Google Drive (drive.readonly) plus the account e-mail address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Use: the application lists and downloads documents from a small number of the owner's own Drive folders and keeps copies on the owner's computer.
  • It never creates, edits, deletes, moves or shares Drive files.
  • Storage: data stays on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored there encrypted.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • Revocation: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Advisor OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
